One plan for everything you're creating, across SEO and PR.

Ask for a full strategy and it builds one from what Searchscope already holds about you: your objectives, your search history, your findings, your competitors, and how AI engines describe your brand. There's no brief to write, because the brief is already in the account.

It proposes the pieces worth making and you keep the ones you agree with. Nothing is created until you say so. Each piece you keep hands off to [Content Studio](/platform/instruments/content-studio/) to build, carrying its shape and its target page with it.

Tracking carries on after publication, including whether AI engines start citing the piece. A plan that can't tell you which of its own suggestions worked will keep suggesting the same things.

The [Content Planner](/platform/instruments/content-planner/) sits with the rest of the instruments.
