When buyers ask ChatGPT or Perplexity a question, some brands get named. Most don't.

The overview gives you that read at a glance: your score, how often you're named and cited, your share of voice, and where you sit inside the answer rather than simply whether you appear in it.

Share of voice is the number worth arguing about. Measured against a list of competitors you typed in, it mostly tells you about your own assumptions. This one is measured against the brands that actually turned up in the answers, which is frequently not the list you'd have written.

Each figure also shows its denominator. Named in 14 of 60 answers, cited in 6 of 14. When a score moves you can see which half of it moved, and you can open the answers underneath rather than taking the number on trust.

It's the front page of [AI and Brand Intelligence](/platform/ai-brand-intelligence/).
