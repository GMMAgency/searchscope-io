Every reply from the Strategic Advisor now carries a Send to Studio action.

The useful context in an advisor thread is rarely in the last message. It's spread across the question you asked, the numbers it pulled back, and the two follow-ups where you narrowed things down. So the whole conversation travels, and Content Studio opens with the reasoning intact rather than one paragraph out of context.

When a reply recommends several things at once, say updating a page and pitching a publication, it splits them into separate pieces you pick from. Each gets its own shape and its own target page. A page update and a press pitch want different drafts, and arriving as one draft is how you end up rewriting both.

The Strategic Advisor is part of [Search Intelligence](/platform/search-intelligence/).
