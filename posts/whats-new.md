We ship most weeks and almost nobody found out. There's now a What's new button at the foot of the sidebar, a card for each update with a picture of the thing, and a small marker on anything you haven't seen yet.

## One card at a time, newest first

The panel steps through updates rather than scrolling them. Each card gets room for a picture, which is the part that makes it worth opening, and stepping onto a card is what marks it read. A list would let you scroll past five entries in half a second and count all five as read, which is how a "new" marker stops meaning anything.

There's a line in the panel showing where your memory stops, so if you've been away a fortnight you know exactly where to stop reading.

## When something stops being new

Not on a timer. A thing stops being new when you've seen it.

A marker in the navigation clears when you visit the page, because arriving proves the marker did its job. A marker on a filter inside a workspace clears only when you use that filter, because arriving on the page proves nothing about a pill on a row you've walked past for months. Using one of three new filters clears one of three.

A calendar cap sits behind all of it so nothing stays flagged forever, but it's the backstop rather than the rule.

## If you're new, nothing is flagged

Someone who joined this week gets the button and the full history, with no markers anywhere. Nothing is new to someone for whom everything is, and a new account is already the most crowded screen in the product without a badge on half the navigation.

## Every update has a home here

Each card links to its post, which is where the detail lives. The panel tells you something shipped and roughly what it does; the post explains it properly. You're reading one now.
