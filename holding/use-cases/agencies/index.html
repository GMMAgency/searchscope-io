01 · The accounts nobody's looked at

The quiet clients are the ones to worry about.

Every agency has accounts that get proper attention, and accounts that get a rankings export. The second group isn't quiet because it's healthy. It's quiet because nobody has looked.

Decay and cannibalization don't announce themselves. A page slips for the queries that earned it, a second page starts competing with the first, and by the time it surfaces in a monthly report your client has already formed a view about what they're paying you for.

02 · Deliver across every account

One pipeline. Every account. Every run.

Most agencies run deep analysis on the account that's complaining, and a monthly rankings export on everything else. Not because anyone thinks that's right, but because reading forty sites properly is more hours than the retainers pay for.

Searchscope runs the same analysis on every client site you've connected, on the same schedule, whether or not anyone asked. Each finding is scored and ranked against the pages that matter commercially, and arrives with the fix already drafted, so the junior work is the review rather than the writing.

03 · Prove the work

Show clients what their retainer actually produced.

The reporting problem in an agency isn't making a chart. It's that the person reading the report is a marketing manager who has to justify your invoice to a finance director who has never heard of a canonical tag.

Searchscope reports say what changed, what was done about it and what it was worth, in that order, in language your client can forward without translating first. Export as a PDF or a shareable Worksheet link, on your schedule.

04 · Running the agency

Client to client, in one click.

Move between accounts without re-orientating yourself every time. Each client's data, findings and recommendations are already in context, so your team can see where attention is needed across the portfolio.

Reports go out on a schedule you set, written from the same live numbers the platform runs on.
